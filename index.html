We don't just advise. We partner.

For startups we work closely with, CyphrWeb becomes a long-term growth partner — contributing expertise, resources, network, technology and marketing support in exchange for a fair, mutually agreed stake in what we help build.

Connect With the Right Network

CyphrWeb works with angel investors who back startups in the ecosystem. Here's how an investor introduction actually happens.

  1. 01

    Build

    You work with CyphrWeb and build real traction — product, users or early revenue.

  2. 02

    Get Reviewed

    Our team and mentors look at where your startup stands and whether it's ready for investor conversations.

  3. 03

    Warm Introduction

    If there's a genuine fit, we introduce you to relevant angel investors in our network.

  4. 04

    Your Conversation

    From there, the conversation and any decision to invest is between you and the investor.

An introduction is a warm connection to the right people, not a promise — CyphrWeb does not guarantee funding or any specific outcome.

Frequently asked questions.

The questions we get asked most in the community — answered upfront.

Do I need a startup idea to join CyphrWeb?

No. Most people who join don't have one yet. Come in to learn, meet people and explore problems worth solving — the idea can come later.

Is joining the CyphrWeb community free?

Yes, joining the community is free. If CyphrWeb becomes a long-term growth partner for your startup, terms are discussed and agreed individually with your founding team — nothing is fixed upfront.

What does a CyphrWeb startup partnership actually involve?

For startups we work closely with, CyphrWeb contributes expertise, resources, network, technology and marketing support in exchange for a fair, mutually agreed stake in what we help build. Every partnership is discussed individually — there's no obligation until both sides are comfortable moving forward.

How do I get matched with a co-founder or team?

Use the "Find a team for your startup" section to register as a founder looking for a co-founder, or as someone who wants to join a team. Share your listing in the community group so more people can see it.

How does an investor introduction work?

Once you're working with CyphrWeb and have built real traction, our team and mentors review whether you're ready for investor conversations. If there's a genuine fit, we make a warm introduction to relevant angel investors — the conversation and any decision from there is between you and the investor.

I'm a developer, marketer or designer — can I join without a startup?

Yes. Use the Talent Registration form in the Careers section to register independently, and we'll match you to the right team.
